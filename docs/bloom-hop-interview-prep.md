## 2. Bloom — Produto em Detalhe

### Visao Geral
Bloom e a plataforma clinica integrada da Sword Health para saude da mulher, cobrindo 5 fases de vida com AI + suporte clinico continuo.

### Proposta de Valor Core
- Substitui solucoes fragmentadas (point solutions) por um sistema clinico unico
- Acesso 24/7 a cuidados clinicos + AI sem espera por consultas
- Outcome-based pricing — clientes so pagam quando resultado clinico positivo e atingido

### 5 Life Stages

**1. Fertility & Preconception**
- Otimizacao hormonal e de ciclo
- Suporte de movimento para saude pelvica
- Readiness metabolica e lifestyle
- Pathways para cuidado especializado

**2. Pregnancy**
- Movimento e estabilidade pelvica por trimestre
- Gestao de dor, sono e fadiga
- Orientacao nutricional
- Suporte gravidez de alto risco (coming soon)

**3. Postpartum (arriving 2026)**
- Recuperacao pavimento pelvico e core
- Recuperacao cicatriz e cesareana
- Orientacao nutricional para equilibrio hormonal
- Suporte lactacao

**4. Menopause/Midlife**
- Gestao hormonal e de sintomas
- Suporte sono, humor, stress
- Protecao saude ossea, cardiaca, metabolica
- Exercicios corpo inteiro + pavimento pelvico

**5. Persistent Pelvic Conditions**
- Exercicios pavimento pelvico
- Fortalecimento corpo inteiro
- Tecnicas mente-corpo e comportamentais
- Educacao lifestyle

### Diferenciacoes Chave
- **Always-On Care:** Acesso continuo a clinicians + AI, sem esperar por consultas
- **Clinical Memory:** Contexto clinico mantido across life stage transitions
- **AI + Clinician Coordination:** AI retains context + surfaces signals + engages proactively; clinicians design care plans + adjust progression + control escalations
- **Device & Behavioral Signals:** Cycle tracking, sleep, stress, activity → intervencoes proactivas
- **Outcome-Based Pricing:** Alinhamento financeiro com resultados

### Metricas Publicadas
- 2.9:1 gross ROI
- $3,082 annual savings per member
- 9/10 member satisfaction
- Revenue 2024 equivalente a toda a empresa em 2022

### Problema de Mercado Que Resolve
- 1 em 8 mulheres tem dificuldade em conceber
- $6.6B custo anual de cesareanas desnecessarias
- 2 em 3 mulheres tem problemas fisicos pos-parto
- $26B custo anual de sintomas de menopausa

### Target Audience
- Employers (self-insured)
- Health plans
- Unions
- Consultants e brokers
- Members (women — utilizadoras finais)

## 4. O Que E Ser Head of Product

### Head of Product vs. Senior PM vs. VP Product

| Dimensao | Senior PM | Head of Product | VP Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foco** | Produto/feature especifica | Funcao de produto inteira | Portfolio de produtos |
| **Equipa** | IC ou mentoring informal | 1-5 PMs diretos | Directors, Group PMs |
| **Horizonte** | Sprint/quarter | 6-12 meses | 12-36 meses |
| **Papel** | Executar + some strategy | Estratega + implementador | Organizational design |
| **Reports to** | HoP ou VP | VP ou CEO | CEO ou CPO |
| **Melhor para** | Produtos em execucao | Startups a construir funcao | Growth-stage com multiplos produtos |

### Competencias Chave de um Head of Product
1. **Visao estrategica** — definir direcao e inspirar a organizacao
2. **Lideranca de pessoas** — construir, mentoring, avaliar equipa de PMs
3. **Cross-functional leadership** — alinhar eng, design, clinical, marketing, sales
4. **Analytical depth** — data-driven decision-making, KPI definition
5. **Technical credibility** — profundidade tecnica suficiente para engajar com eng
6. **Communication** — traduzir entre executive leadership e equipas tecnicas
7. **User empathy** — advocacy pelo utilizador final em todas as fases
8. **Business acumen** — financial, GTM, pricing, partnership awareness
9. **Ambiguity navigation** — operar em contextos incertos e em mudanca
10. **Team development** — atrair, reter, e desenvolver talento PM

## 5. Competencias Health Tech

### Regulatory Frameworks

**HIPAA**
- Regula privacidade e seguranca de Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Aplica-se a covered entities e business associates
- Requer encriptacao, access controls, audit trails, breach notification

**FDA Digital Health**
- Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) — requer safety e effectiveness demonstradas
- Device classifications: Class I (lower risk), Class II (510(k)), Class III (PMA)
- Clinical Decision Support software — transparencia sobre funcionalidade e limitacoes
- Sword Thrive e FDA-listed

**Clinical Evidence**
- Gold standard: Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs)
- Alternativas: Real-World Data (RWD), Real-World Evidence (RWE), digital biomarkers
- Sword: 29+ papers peer-reviewed, 57 estudos clinicos

### B2B2C em Health Tech
- **Modelo:** Digital health company → employer/health plan (payer) → employee/member (user)
- **Porque employers usam:** reduzir custos de saude, melhorar satisfacao, analytics
- **Challenges para product leaders:** sales cycles longos, employee adoption, HIPAA, integracao HR systems, multiple user types
- **Mercado:** ~153M pessoas com employer-sponsored coverage nos US

### Outcome-Based Pricing
- Pagamento ligado a resultados clinicos reais (nao fee-for-service)
- Sword: 50% activation + 50% outcome (Patient Global Impression of Change scale)
- Transfere risco para o provider — alinhamento total de incentivos
- Implicacao para produto: cada feature tem de demonstravelmente melhorar clinical outcomes

### Colaboracao com Equipas Clinicas
- **Fase de desenvolvimento:** Clinical advisors validam problem statement, workflows, prototipos
- **Evidencia clinica:** Colaboracao com researchers para RCTs, definicao de endpoints
- **Evolucao continua:** Feedback loops com clinical users, clinical advisory board
- **Regulatory:** Input clinico em FDA submissions, labeling, risk management
- **Equipa tipica Bloom:** PTs, NPs, OB-GYNs, physicians, clinical researchers, health outcome specialists

### Metricas em Health Tech
- **Engagement:** activation rate, session frequency, feature adoption
- **Retention:** member retention, churn, program completion
- **Clinical Outcomes:** Patient Global Impression of Change, pain reduction, functional improvement
- **Business:** ROI per member, cost savings, NPS/satisfaction
- **Bloom specifico:** 2.9:1 ROI, $3,082 savings/member, 9/10 satisfaction

## 8. Estrategia de Mitigacao de Gaps

### Gap: 3+ anos de lideranca formal

**Mitigacao:**
- Framing: "I have 1.5 years of **building** PM culture from zero — Product Talks, mentoring programme, practice adoption across 3+ teams. Building is harder than inheriting."
- Evidencia concreta: Product Talks (6 PMs, 3-weekly cadence, 1 year), Beatriz (6 months structured development programme, direct report), Epic Refinement spread to 3 teams.
- Counter: "In a flat organization, leadership is about influence and impact, not reporting lines. I chose to create a PM community — no one asked me to."

### Gap: Track record B2C digital

**Mitigacao:**
- NOS Self-Service App (B2C, inception-to-launch, 10 months, Deloitte)
- BookVault (shipped, active, B2C, personal project)
- PlateCheck (health/nutrition, in progress, B2C)
- ONE is functionally B2B2C — agents in dealerships are end users, BMW brands are the payer. Same model as Bloom.

### Gap: Startup/high-growth

**Mitigacao:**
- "Operating in ambiguity inside a 100K+ employee company has its own kind of chaos — competing agendas, political escalations, no clear owner. I've navigated that for 4 years."
- "I've shown I can build things from zero — Product Talks, PM culture, even personal shipped apps. I don't need a startup playbook to move fast."
- Technical literacy (builds with AI, ships code) signals startup-compatible hands-on-ness.

### Gap: US healthcare market

**Mitigacao:**
- "Platform scaling is platform scaling — I've coordinated rollout across 73 countries and 30+ markets. Geographic market knowledge is acquirable; platform thinking is not."
- Clinical domain knowledge (biology, nursing, reproductive health) transfers regardless of geography.
- Admit honestly: "I would need to learn US payer dynamics. But my learning speed is demonstrated — I went from zero to owning 80% of product direction in 4 years."

### Gap: Clinical outcomes experience

**Mitigacao:**
- Biology degree + nursing year = clinical foundations
- PCOS personal experience = deep user empathy
- "I've never built a clinical outcomes product — but I've built products where metrics had to justify every decision (Translation API: €80K saved; Notification analysis: 91% vanity metric debunked). The discipline of evidence-based decision-making is the same."
- Strong learning signal: "I know what a clinical endpoint is. I know what a p-value means. I've read Sword's published studies."

### Gap: Regulated industry (healthcare-specific)

**Mitigacao:**
- Automotive has compliance requirements (homologation, safety standards, market-specific regulations across 73 countries)
- "Regulatory compliance isn't about knowing the specific rules — it's about building processes where compliance is embedded in development, not bolted on. I've done that at BMW scale."
- Willingness to learn HIPAA, FDA digital health frameworks explicitly.
